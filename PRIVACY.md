# Privacy Policy for Ormayundo

This Privacy Policy describes how Ormayundo ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our Browser Extension.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** We collect your email address and an encrypted password when you create an account to authenticate you.
- **Usage Data:** We collect the specific text you highlight and choose to save, as well as the source URL of the web page, in order to generate AI flashcards. 
- **Device Information:** We securely store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token to send you spaced repetition push notifications directly to your desktop.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We strictly use your information to provide the core functionality of the extension (generating AI flashcards and scheduling review notifications). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 3. Data Security
Your data is securely transmitted over HTTPS and stored in MongoDB Atlas. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using bcrypt before storage.

## 4. Your Rights
You maintain full control over your data. You can delete any flashcard individually, or entirely wipe your flashcard database at any time using the delete buttons inside the extension dashboard.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository.
